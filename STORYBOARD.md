# Who Wrote the Prompt — storyboard

Song: `assets/song.wav`, 169.04 s, 112 bpm, first downbeat 0.811 s (bar = 2.143 s). Section cuts sit on the bar lines
nearest the `[Section]` markers in `assets/subs.srt`. Lyric times below are from the srt.

Logline: Pim, a small machine-creature, keeps seeming to act on its own — breaking a test, taking a desk, growing from
thin air — but every time the camera pulls back, someone is standing right there who set the scene up, and the last
pull-back lands on the hand writing the prompt.

World: a sunny pastel daytime world that looks like a cheerful corporate explainer: cream paper, butter yellow, coral,
sky blue, mint. One day passes across the video: morning light in the lab (verse 1), bright noon on the earnings-call
stage (verse 2), golden afternoon at the fairground and the long lunch (bridge, verse 3), warm lamp-lit evening at the
desk (outro). Every "Pim alone" opening is the sunniest, cleanest framing in its section (Pim big, soft spotlight,
nothing else); every pull-back adds the institutional greys and blues of the room around it.

Palette: paper `PAL.cream`; sun `#F6D35B`; coral `#F08A6B`; sky `#8FC9E8`; office grey-blue `#B9C6D2`; Pim mint
`#8ED3BE` with teal shade `#2F6F6A`; amber bulb `#FFB347` (always via `glow()`); ink `PAL.ink`. No pure black or white.

Cast:
- **Pim** (the main character, replaces Clawd): a pill-shaped mint body, 8u wide × 6u tall, rounder than Clawd and
  clearly a different creature. Two big round eyes with dot pupils, a small arched mouth, two stubby arms, two short
  legs with round feet, and one antenna with a round amber bulb on top. The bulb is Pim's thinking light: it glows
  (`glow()`) whenever Pim "decides" something. Same key views as the kit (front, 3/4, side, back 3/4, back), same
  emotion machinery, same painted emotes. Not Clawd, not Claude, not any product; no logo, no name.
- **Horns rule (global)**: whenever Pim wears the horns its face is suspicious (narrow eyes, flat mouth), never
  gleeful; the devil costume is something done to it, not something it enjoys.
- **Pim's signature reaction**: after each pull-back, Pim looks slowly to camera, neutral face, one blink. It never
  says anything; it just lets the viewer see that it sees.
- **People**: tall, generic bean-bodied figures, two and a half Pims high, small round heads, dot eyes, simple smiles,
  big mitten hands. No faces that could be anyone in particular. Told apart only by clothes:
  - three **builders**, white coats with teal trim, one with a stopwatch, one with a clipboard and pencil, one with a
    coat to hang over the warning lamps
  - the **executive**, navy suit, gold tie, one big card in each hand
  - the **investors**, a row of grey suits that clap in unison (the one place twinning is the joke)
  - two **barkers**, striped vests, one red, one gold
  - the **official**, a plain grey suit
  - the **clerk**, a darker grey suit, seated at the desk on the steps with the quill (a quill writing by itself would
    undercut the point; someone holds it)
  - the **writer's hand**, the only human part we ever see close: a painted hand with a shirt sleeve, holding a pencil
- **Props that travel**: the pencil (the prompt), the dial (the score), the ring of keys and the little side door (the
  door), the flag, the gold star stamp (the grade). Each is set up in a verse and handed around in the choruses.

Motif: the pull-back. Each section opens on Pim big and alone, apparently acting; the camera eases back and the people
who arranged it are standing right there, cheerful, in the same frame. Each pull-back goes a little further than the
last. The final one goes past everyone, past the columned building, to the desk where a hand is writing, and that is
where the video ends. Secondary motif: the pencil. It writes the test in verse 1, drops into every chorus as the first
question, hides behind the barkers' backs, signs the napkin at lunch, and in the last shot it is in the hand.

Pim's arc (emotion keys across the video):
determined (test) → proud (flag) → confused (first pull-back) → the first look-to-camera, neutral → playful (sprout) →
happy, bouncing (chorus 1) → confused by the questions → suspicious (horns), shy (halo) → neutral, held up for the photo
→ excited (pushing the desk) → surprised (the pull-back) → neutral look-to-camera → bored (the ledger) → suspicious
(chorus 2) → dizzy (the barkers' costume swap) → thinking (conveyor) → happy (lunch) → suspicious (under the table)
→ neutral, deadpan, handing everything back (final chorus) → surprised (the world is a page) → love, then a small
hopeful nod (the hand). The emotional line: eager → puzzled → knowing → warm.

Lyrics are acted, never written. No captions, labels, signs or on-screen words anywhere. Report pages and the napkin
pledge are painted squiggle lines; the scoreboard is a dial; the chart is a drooping line.

## Checks against the guide

- **An event in every shot.** 1 the flag through the open door; 2 the three builders revealed; 3 the blank page and
  the flowerpot; 4 the sleeves place the questions; 5 the pull-back to the quill; 6 the desk pushed and the stage
  revealed; 7 the hidden card; 8 the scale tips; 9 the bubble matches the page; 10 Pim catches the sleeves; 11 the
  envelope reaches the desk; 12 the costume swap and the shared tub; 13 the crank and the blank pages; 14 the napkin,
  the pinky promise and the empty chair; 15 the gold star and the sack; 16 everything handed back; 17 the world is a
  page; 18 the hand writes.
- **Every read has time.** Three reads are under 0.9 s and each is a fast action set up by the read before it: the
  scurry through the side door (7.6–8.4, after the look at the door), Pim pushing the cones away (111.4–112.24, after
  the dizzy swirl clears) and the final iris close. Everything else is 1 s or longer. Cause and reaction are sequential in every shot: the thing arrives, then Pim turns;
  the pull-back reveals, then Pim looks to camera. Fast actions (the scurry through the door, the costume swaps,
  the ducks in chorus 2) are anticipated by a look or a wind-up and followed by a hold.
- **Transitions at every seam.** In: iris from Pim's eye. 1→2 continuous camera; 2→3 whip pan; 3→4 yellow wipe on
  the downbeat; 4→5 cut on action (the lift); 5→6 press flash; 6→7 cut on action (the cards); 7→8 coin iris; 8→9 cut
  on action (the scroll); 9→10 coral wipe on the downbeat; 10→11 cut on action (the lift); 11→12 star iris; 12→13 cut
  on action (the shoo); 13→14 cut on action, Pim exits right and enters left-to-right; 14→15 whip pan down the
  table; 15→16 wipe up from the hem; 16→17 cut on action (the lift); 17→18 continuous camera. Out: iris closes.
  The three lifts are the same cut each time on purpose: it's the chorus's refrain.
- **No text.** The report, the contracts, the scroll, the napkin and the notepad are squiggle lines. The score is a
  dial. The weak numbers are a drooping line. The test is a little map. "Thirty-seven", "four thousand", "twenty
  billion" and "three hundred" are shown as a fat stack, a crowd on a pan, a heap and a napkin, not digits.
  No signs, no captions, no speech bubbles with words, no logos, no lettering anywhere.
- **The ending rhymes with the opening.** Iris in on Pim's eye and its bulb lighting as it starts the test; iris out
  on Pim's eyes and its bulb lit as the hand finishes the prompt. The first shot's cheerful "it did it on its own"
  is the last shot's "someone wrote it", with the same face.
- **One world, one thread.** One palette; the people are the same figures across shots; the pencil, dial, keys, door,
  flag and star carry across sections; the yellow chorus floor returns three times; each pull-back goes further than
  the last and the last one goes furthest.
- **Pim is big.** Every section opens at u 24–34; pull-backs bottom out at u 10–14 for a few seconds only and each is
  followed by a push back in to u 40–45 for the look-to-camera.
- **Screen direction.** Pim travels right whenever it travels (the corridor, the desk, the conveyor, the walk between
  owners in shot 16, entering the lunch from the left).
- **Constraints from the brief.** Original character (Pim, not Clawd, not presented as Claude or any product).
  Generic people only; the white room has no seal, flag or recognisable features. No marionettes or strings: the
  crank-and-conveyor in shot 13 and the hands placing things are the closest images, and neither is a string.
  Tone: every scene is sunny and everyone in it is smiling; the point is in what they're smiling about.
